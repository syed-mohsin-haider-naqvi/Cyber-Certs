# SOC Analyst Essentials — Udemy

## About This Course

Shorter course then the cisco ones, more of a quick overview style thing rather then a deep structured program. Found it on udemy while looking for something to fill in gaps between the bigger courses. Its basically a fast tour through SOC concepts, common tools, and some networking basics, wrapped up with an actual lecture on searching for job positions on linkedin which was a nice practical touch most courses dont bother including.

Took this one after already having some of the cisco courses under my belt so alot of it was reinforcing stuff i knew, but the tool specific lectures (SIEM, SOAR, EDR, IDS/IPS) were more focused then how cisco covered similar concepts, since this course is specifically SOC-tool oriented rather then general networking/security.

**Status:** Completed

---

## What It Covered

Course is broken into short lectures, mostly 3-6 minutes each, grouped loosely by topic:

- What a SOC actually is, roles within cybersecurity generally, and how a SOC is structured
- Types of hackers, the CIA triad again (keeps coming up across basically every course, which says something about how central it actually is), and Zero Trust as a concept
- The core SOC tools — SIEM, SOAR, firewalls, EDR, IDS/IPS, plus a recap tying them together
- IP addressing, DNS, phishing, and OSINT
- Linux basics and malware, quickly
- Course slides, and then a practical lecture actually searching for SOC job positions on linkedin, plus a bonus lecture at the end

Quizzes show up after each topic cluster to check retention along the way.

---

## What Actually Stuck

The SIEM/SOAR/EDR/IDS-IPS lectures were the most useful part honestly, even though each ones only a few minutes long. Cisco courses mention these tools but this course spends dedicated time on each one specifically, which helped me actually keep them straight — SIEM collects and correlates logs, SOAR automates response actions, EDR is endpoint focused detection/response, IDS just detects while IPS actually blocks. Small distinctions but ones i used to blur together before this.

The linkedin job search lecture was random for a technical course to include but genuinely useful — walking through what SOC job postings actually look like and what they ask for helped me figure out what to actually prioritize learning next, rather then just guessing.
